## Permission route

Obtain the delivered source file or session, stems, composer and publisher details, recording owner and performers, and the recipe for every sample, voice, loop, preset and SoundFont that appears in the master. Match the approved master to the shipped hash or document the export chain. Verify written rights for composition and recording, including synchronization, reproduction and commercial distribution in the game. Record territories, term, edit/loop rights, required credits and any trailer or listing usage separately. Start with the supplying contributor and keep contracts in protected storage. [U.S. Copyright Office on composition and recording](https://www.copyright.gov/register/pa-sr.html)
